The legal text is included as `CC0-1.0.txt`.

VINDecoded derivatives retain the geometry, bake coral/teal/cream colors into
linear vertex colors, normalize the ground-centred origin, merge surfaces and
apply Meshopt compression. `manifest.json` records the exact source URLs and
SHA-256 hashes, derived hashes, dimensions and triangle counts. No source
textures, rig, animation or remote runtime dependencies are used.

Reproduction and scene placement: `pacific-overdrive/docs/beach-assets.md` in the
source checkout; importer: `scripts/import-beach-assets.mjs`.
