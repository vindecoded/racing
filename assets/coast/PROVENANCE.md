# Sunset Run CC0 asset palette

Retrieved 2026-10-09 from the creators' official distribution endpoints.
`manifest.json` records every shipped runtime file, its SHA-256 and byte count,
the exact original URL and SHA-256, authors and transformations.

- **Asphalt Track** — Dimitrios Savva, Poly Haven.
  <https://polyhaven.com/a/asphalt_track>
- **Rock Boulder Dry** — Dimitrios Savva (photography), Rico Cilliers (processing), Poly Haven.
  <https://polyhaven.com/a/rock_boulder_dry>
- **Racing Kit** — Kenney. Only `barrierRed.glb`, `barrierWhite.glb` and
  `pylon.glb` from `Models/GLTF format/` are distributed here, unchanged.
  <https://kenney.nl/assets/racing-kit>

Poly Haven identifies its downloadable textures, HDRIs and models as CC0:
<https://polyhaven.com/license>. Kenney's original archive license is preserved
verbatim in `KENNEY-LICENSE.txt`. The CC0 1.0 legal text is preserved in
`CC0-1.0.txt`, retrieved from
<https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/legalcode.txt>.
CC0 permits commercial use and redistribution. Credits are retained for provenance.
No provider logos, preview renders, website artwork or asset-pack promotional
images are included.

The original Kenney ZIP and source 1K textures are acquisition inputs, not
runtime downloads. The game serves only these local optimized files. See
`../../../docs/coast-assets.md` for art direction and the loader contract.

Archived (no longer shipped): **Kiara 1 Dawn** — Greg Zaal, Poly Haven, CC0
(<https://polyhaven.com/a/kiara_1_dawn>). Its 512×256 RGBE derivative
(`kiara_1_dawn-512.hdr`, 529,457 bytes, SHA-256
`d6421ec13fb3c29338b729e7de5b8473bddfd8abb2806afb78a60ccc706ca13f`, from
`https://dl.polyhaven.org/file/ph-assets/HDRIs/hdr/1k/kiara_1_dawn_1k.hdr`,

was removed once reflections were generated from the course sky.
